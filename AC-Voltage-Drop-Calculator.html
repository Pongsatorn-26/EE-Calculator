The reactive term uses sin φ, calculated from your power factor as φ = arccos(PF), so sin φ = √(1 − PF²). Bundle is the number of parallel conductors per phase, and the drop is divided by it (Bundle 1 gives the single-cable result). Percentage drop is the voltage drop divided by the system voltage.
